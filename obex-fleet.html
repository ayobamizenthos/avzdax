Modern fleet cameras generally fall into two categories: front facing cameras that monitor the road ahead, and dual facing cameras like the OBEX Fleet Dual View that monitor both the driver cabin and the forward road. Selecting the right configuration depends on your fleet risk profile, with dual view units providing the most complete liability protection and predictive driver safety capabilities.

Traditional dash cams only record events after they happen, serving as passive digital witnesses. Predictive mobility intelligence systems like OBEX Fleet actively analyze driver behavior such as fatigue, distraction, and trailing distance in real time. By delivering instantaneous in cab audio alerts, they allow drivers to correct behavior before an incident occurs, reducing collision rates by up to 50% and protecting fleets from catastrophic liability.

A traditional dash cam functions as a simple loop recording camera. An AI enabled system utilizes an integrated neural processing unit to run computer vision models on the edge. This enables the device to detect complex events such as drowsiness, mobile phone distraction, lane departures, and rolling stops and automatically upload high priority event footage to the cloud while keeping standard footage private.

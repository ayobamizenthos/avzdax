AI that runs on your cameras, not in distant data centers

Traditional surveillance routes video through centralized cloud servers. This creates latency, bandwidth bottlenecks, and external dependencies that compromise response speed and data sovereignty. When connectivity fails, these systems fail with it.

PRIMUS relocates intelligence directly to the point of observation. Each node embeds autonomous neural processing that analyzes video locally, identifies behavioral anomalies, and initiates responses without external communication. Your footage never leaves your infrastructure.

Designed to work universally. PRIMUS connects to legacy video feeds, instantly upgrading passive recording devices into active, predictive sensors. It scales effortlessly across facilities, ensuring consistent security for every site.
